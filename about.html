Our Purpose

We treat the whole person,
not just the illness

Golden State Rehab is an outpatient addiction and mental health treatment center in Los Angeles, and our mission is to help people reclaim their lives by offering thoughtful, individualized treatment rooted in respect, honesty, and clinical expertise. We walk beside every client through healing, growth, and long-term recovery, ensuring no one has to navigate this journey alone.

Golden State Rehab exists to guide people toward lasting recovery with compassion and clinical expertise. We believe healing is possible for everyone who seeks it, and we're here to walk that path with you.

Our Approach

Healing requires both science and humanity

We believe in meeting people with respect, treating addiction and mental health as interconnected challenges that deserve integrated care.

Evidence-Based Care

Every treatment approach we use is grounded in research and clinical best practices. We combine the latest evidence with genuine compassion.

Integrated Treatment

We address mental health and addiction together, recognizing that these challenges are deeply interconnected and require unified care.

Flexible Scheduling

Our programs are designed around your life — not the other way around. From morning PHP to evening IOP, we fit recovery into your schedule.

Our Distinction

We stand apart through depth and dedication

Golden State Rehab has earned Joint Commission's Gold Seal of Approval®, the national benchmark for quality and safety in health care. We are also DHCS-licensed by the State of California (License #191643AP — verify it in the DHCS directory), meeting the leading national standards for behavioral health care.

Our clinical team brings decades of combined experience in addiction medicine and psychiatric care, with therapists who specialize in trauma-informed treatment. We are committed to offering integrated programs that address both mental health and substance use together — an approach many facilities still treat separately.

We are also deliberately connected beyond our own walls. When a client needs medical detox before starting with us, we arrange it with licensed partners and hold their spot. When structured housing would strengthen recovery, we coordinate referrals to established sober living operators across Southern California, such as Comeback Sober Living. And when treatment ends, our alumni network keeps the community around you.
