  1. Our certification, #191643AP

    April 15, 2026

    In April, the State of California made it official

    The Department of Health Care Services is the state agency that certifies outpatient rehabs here, and on April 15 they approved ours. That's the day we could actually start helping people.

  2. May 26, 2026

    Six weeks later, we opened our doors to our first clients

    Btw, we're at 1964 Westwood Blvd, Suite 425, right in the middle of Westwood.

  3. June 2026

    In June, we got our very first Google review

    Read the reviews
  4. Gold Seal of Approval

    August 12, 2026

    In August, we were accredited by the same group that inspects hospitals

    It's called The Joint Commission, an independent nonprofit that checks healthcare organizations all over the country. They gave us their Gold Seal of Approval eleven weeks after we opened.

    Our listing

  5. September 2026

    In September, Nechama and Eduardo joined, and that made nine of us

    Nechama is a trauma-informed therapist and Eduardo is our program director. All nine of us still work out of that same suite in Westwood.
