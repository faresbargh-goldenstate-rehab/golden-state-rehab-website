What Kind of Rehabilitation Center We Are

Real Rehab, Without Moving Into a Facility

Golden State Rehab is an outpatient drug and alcohol rehabilitation center — you come in for structured treatment during the day or evening, then go home to your own bed. There are no overnight stays, no locked doors, and no six-week disappearance from your life. And because addiction rarely travels alone, our clinicians treat the substance use and the co-occurring depression, anxiety, or trauma underneath it in the same treatment plan, not in separate silos.

To be clear on terms: this is a behavioral health rehabilitation center for addiction and mental health, not a physical or medical rehab facility for injuries, stroke, or surgery recovery. As a Los Angeles addiction treatment center, we offer a full ladder of outpatient care — from full weekdays of clinical programming down to a few evening sessions a week — so treatment can start intensive and loosen as your recovery strengthens. Every level is built from the same ingredients: group therapy led by licensed clinicians, weekly individual sessions with your primary therapist, and psychiatric evaluation and medication support when your plan calls for it.

Everything happens at our Westwood center at 1964 Westwood Blvd, Ste 425, or through secure telehealth anywhere in California. One phone call is enough to figure out where you'd start.

A Step-Down Pathway

Levels of Care at Our Rehabilitation Center

Partial Hospitalization (PHP)

Our most structured level — full weekdays of therapy, psychiatry, and skill-building at the center, with your evenings and nights at home. PHP is where treatment usually begins when you need the most support, or where you land after finishing residential care elsewhere.

Intensive Outpatient (IOP)

The middle rung: 3-hour sessions several days a week, so treatment stays substantial while work, school, or family life resumes around it. Most clients step down into IOP from PHP — or start here when full days aren't clinically necessary.

Evening IOP

The same IOP curriculum on an after-work clock — 3-hour evening groups, 3 to 5 nights a week. Evening IOP exists for people whose daytime obligations are non-negotiable: careers to protect, kids to pick up, and no room for a leave of absence.

Virtual IOP

Full intensive outpatient treatment through secure video — live groups, individual sessions, and psychiatry from anywhere in California. Virtual IOP is the same program and the same clinicians, minus the commute across town.

Outpatient Drug Rehab

As you stabilize, hours taper: our outpatient rehab program continues group and individual therapy on a lighter weekly schedule. It's the bridge between intensive treatment and ordinary life — structure that loosens as your recovery holds.

Telehealth Rehab

Every level of our care can flex online. Telehealth rehab covers secure video therapy and psychiatry statewide — useful when you live outside the Westside, travel for work, or simply do your best talking from your own kitchen table.

Licensed, Accredited, Verifiable

Check Our Credentials Before You Trust Us With Anything

Rehab is an industry where you should verify claims, including ours. Here's what you can check independently before you ever pick up the phone:

  • Licensed by the California Department of Health Care Services — DHCS license #191643AP, verifiable in the state's public provider directory
  • Accredited by The Joint Commission, whose Gold Seal marks behavioral health organizations that meet its national standards for safety and quality of care
  • Outpatient-only, and honest about it: we don't operate beds, so there is no residential or inpatient program here — and we'll never pretend otherwise
  • If your situation calls for medical detox or residential care first, we say so plainly and help you find a licensed provider for that step — then treatment continues with us once you're ready for outpatient care

The difference in plain terms: inpatient rehab means living at a facility around the clock; outpatient rehab means structured treatment hours at a center while you live at home. For many people — clinically stable, with a safe place to sleep and reasons to stay rooted in daily life — outpatient is not the lesser option. It's the right one.

Rehabilitation Center: Common Questions

A drug and alcohol rehabilitation center is a licensed facility where people receive structured, clinical treatment for substance use — typically a combination of group therapy, individual therapy, psychiatric care, and relapse-prevention skills, delivered by licensed clinicians. It's different from a physical rehab facility, which treats injuries and surgical recovery. Rehab centers come in two broad forms: inpatient (you live at the facility) and outpatient (you attend treatment hours and live at home).
