Golden State Rehab is licensed by the California Department of Health Care Services. Here is the certificate, and how to check it yourself.

DHCS Licensed

License #191643AP

Golden State Rehab operates under license number 191643AP, issued by the California Department of Health Care Services (DHCS), the state agency that licenses and certifies substance use disorder treatment providers in California.

You do not have to take our word for it. DHCS publishes directories of every licensed and certified facility in the state, so you can look up this license — or any other provider you are considering — directly with the state.
