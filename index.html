Know the people behind your care.

Meet the team at Golden State Rehab in Los Angeles. Learn about their experience in addiction treatment and how each person supports your care.

  • Dr. Eric Chaghouri, MD

    Medical Director

    Board-certified psychiatrist

    Dr. Chaghouri oversees clinical operations at the center and provides psychiatric care and medication management when clients need it. A board-certified psychiatrist who earned his M.D. at USC’s Keck School of Medicine, he focuses on treating addiction alongside co-occurring mental health conditions.

  • Ari Labowitz, LMFT

    Clinical Director

    Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist

    As Clinical Director, Ari leads the therapy programs at Golden State Rehab. A Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist, he treats depression, anxiety, and major life transitions through individual, couples, and family therapy.

  • Viola Sulahian, AMFT

    Primary Therapist

    Associate Marriage and Family Therapist

    Viola works with clients as a primary therapist, treating addiction alongside depression, anxiety, PTSD, and other co-occurring conditions. An Associate Marriage and Family Therapist working toward licensure under supervision, she brings nearly four years of experience across detox, residential, PHP, and IOP.

  • Juanita Casillas, RADT

    AOD Counselor

    Registered Alcohol and Drug Technician

    Juanita facilitates individual and group sessions, helping clients recognize their triggers and build relapse-prevention plans for life after treatment. A Registered Alcohol and Drug Technician with an Associate in Addiction Studies, she has more than four years of experience from detox through outpatient care.

  • Vindell Brunson

    Program Director

    Programming and levels of care

    Vindell oversees how day-to-day programming runs at the center, including how clients move between levels of care. He brings more than 24 years of experience in addiction treatment and 26 years of his own continuous recovery.

  • Dean McDermott

    Director of Operations

    Alcohol and drug counselor, breathwork specialist

    As Director of Operations, Dean oversees the center’s overall operations. An alcohol and drug counselor and breathwork specialist, he brings his own recovery from addiction, and an earlier career as an actor and comedian, to the team.

If you do need time away from work, FMLA can protect your job for up to 12 weeks at employers it covers, and the ADA generally protects people who are in treatment from being fired for it. PHP typically runs 4–6 weeks and IOP 8–12 weeks, though many people step down through levels of care rather than stopping after one. Your timeline gets set with your clinical team, not by a calendar. The research is consistent on one point: outcomes get better the longer someone stays engaged in treatment, and stepping down gradually, from PHP to IOP to outpatient to alumni support, holds up better than stopping the week you first feel steady.
