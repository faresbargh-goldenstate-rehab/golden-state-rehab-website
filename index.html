  1. 01within hours

    First Contact

    You call us or send in the intake form, we answer your questions, and we set up your assessment.

  2. 0260–90 minutes

  4. 04week 1

    You meet your therapist, join your first group, and begin the real work alongside people who understand what you're facing. And recovery doesn't end at discharge — you stay part of our alumni community long after.
