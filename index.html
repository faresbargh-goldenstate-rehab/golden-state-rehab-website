Outpatient Treatment Programs in Los Angeles

Golden State Rehab has two levels of care — structured outpatient treatment programs with intensive clinical care that still leaves room for work, school, and family.

Partial Hospitalization Program (PHP)

Mon–Fri · 9am–3pm · about 30 hours a week

Our most structured level of care — as close to residential as you can get without moving in. Full weekdays in Westwood, home every night.

Ideal for
  • Stepping down from residential or inpatient treatment
  • Dual diagnosis that needs daily clinical support
  • Early recovery where weekly therapy isn’t holding
  • Symptoms disrupting work, school, or home
What you get
  • Daily clinical skills groups — CBT, DBT, and trauma-focused
  • Private sessions with your therapist, plus family therapy
  • Medication management with a board-certified psychiatrist
  • Psychoeducation, holistic sessions, and daily mindfulness
Explore PHP

Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

3–5 days a week · 3-hour sessions · 9–15 hours a week

One step down from PHP, built around a life you can’t put on hold. Morning and evening tracks, in Westwood or by telehealth across California.

Ideal for
  • Clients finishing PHP and ready for more independence
  • Professionals and students who need to keep working
  • Parents and caregivers with responsibilities at home
  • Mild to moderate symptoms with a stable place to live
What you get
  • Group therapy — CBT, DBT, relapse prevention, and trauma work
  • At least one private session a week with your therapist
  • Psychiatric support and MAT when your plan calls for it
  • Family sessions, psychoeducation, and holistic wellness

Licensed outpatient treatment that

Care at our private West Los Angeles center is led by Dr. Eric Chaghouri, MD, a board-certified psychiatrist — with morning and evening tracks built around your life.

Licensed clinicians, never interns

You work directly with licensed therapists, psychiatrists, and certified addiction specialists, under the direction of Medical Director Dr. Eric Chaghouri, MD, who earned his M.D. at the Keck School of Medicine at USC. Care starts with a full clinical assessment, and the plan that comes out of it draws on CBT, DBT, EMDR, and medication-assisted treatment — shaped around your diagnosis, your goals, and your day-to-day life. The same clinicians stay with you from your first call through aftercare.

Morning and evening tracks

A partial hospitalization program (PHP), an intensive outpatient program, telehealth, and individual therapy — scheduled so treatment fits around a job, not instead of one. You live at home and stay in your own community the whole time; see how outpatient drug rehab in Los Angeles works.

DHCS-licensed, Joint Commission accredited

We hold California DHCS License #191643AP and accreditation from The Joint Commission — both public records you can look up. Who walks through our doors

If you see yourself here, you’re in the right place

  • Young adults figuring out recovery and real life at the same time — school, first jobs, and a future still taking shape.

  • Working professionals who need serious treatment that starts after the workday ends — no leave of absence, no explanations.

  • People in early recovery who know weekly therapy isn’t holding, and want more structure before things slip.

  • People carrying both a mental health condition and substance use — tired of being treated for one half at a time.

  • People stepping down from residential care who need a bridge between 24/7 support and standing on their own.

  • Anyone who wants a supportive place to recover — real clinical care and real community, without a hospital stay.

No two of these stories are the same — which is why there is no standard plan here. Your treatment is customized to your needs and your goals, built for a recovery that lasts.
