Using Cigna for Treatment

How Cigna Behavioral Health Benefits Actually Work

Cigna Healthcare is a global insurer, and if your card says Cigna, your addiction and mental health benefits are typically administered through Evernorth Behavioral Health — Cigna's behavioral health arm. That detail matters, because it's Evernorth that reviews authorization requests and pays behavioral claims, not the medical side of your plan. The federal Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act and California's SB 855 both require that these benefits be comparable to your medical coverage, not an afterthought.

Still, the fine print differs from plan to plan: PPO, EPO, HMO, or Open Access Plus, each with its own deductible and network rules. Rather than guess, let us verify your Cigna benefits for free. We'll call on your behalf, usually get answers within the hour, and explain in plain terms how your plan applies to outpatient rehab in Los Angeles. Asking costs nothing and obligates you to nothing.

What Affects Your Coverage

The Details That Decide What Cigna Pays

  • Plan type. Cigna sells PPO, EPO, HMO, and Open Access Plus (OAP) plans. OAP and PPO plans usually include out-of-network benefits; EPO and HMO plans typically don't — and that difference shapes everything else.
  • Deductible status. A plan with a $500 deductible you've already met behaves nothing like a $4,000 deductible in January. We check where you stand today.
  • Out-of-pocket maximum. Once you hit this annual ceiling, your plan generally pays covered costs in full for the rest of the year — sometimes making treatment far more affordable than expected.
  • Prior authorization. Evernorth typically reviews PHP and IOP requests before treatment starts and at intervals during it. We manage those reviews so you don't have to.
  • Medical necessity. Benefits attach to the level of care your assessment supports, which is why a free clinical assessment is always our first step.

Cigna Rehab Coverage: Common Questions

Cigna plans generally include behavioral health benefits administered through Evernorth, and both federal parity law and California's SB 855 require medically necessary addiction treatment to be covered on terms comparable to medical care. Whether your plan applies here — and at what cost to you — depends on its type and network rules, so the practical first step is to verify your benefits for free.

Network relationships change, so we don't publish a list that could be out of date. We work with most major PPO plans, and Cigna PPO and Open Access Plus plans usually include out-of-network benefits. When we verify your coverage, we tell you exactly how your plan applies here — before you commit to anything.

Evernorth Behavioral Health is the Cigna company that typically manages mental health and substance use benefits for Cigna members. It handles the authorization reviews and claims for treatment like PHP and IOP. You don't need to navigate it yourself — our admissions team deals with Evernorth directly as part of verifying and setting up your care.

A denial isn't the end of the road. Internal appeals often succeed, and if they don't, California law entitles you to an independent medical review by the state. We prepare authorization requests carefully the first time, handle the clinical reviews during treatment, and will walk you through appeal options and self-pay alternatives if a denial happens.

Your records are protected by HIPAA and by 42 CFR Part 2, the federal confidentiality rule written specifically for substance use treatment. Verifying benefits doesn't notify your employer, and we never contact them. If privacy around work is a concern, our evening IOP track and telehealth options let treatment fit quietly around your schedule.
