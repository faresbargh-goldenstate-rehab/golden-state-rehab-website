What does benzodiazepine addiction treatment involve?

Two things, side by side. Our physicians lower your dose slowly, often over weeks to months, and therapy treats the anxiety and insomnia the pills were covering up. It all happens outpatient in Westwood, so most people keep working while they recover.

Sources: NIDA on prescription CNS depressants and its DrugFacts page.

I take it exactly as prescribed. Can I still be dependent?

Yes. The FDA's boxed warning on benzodiazepines says physical dependence can develop even at prescribed doses, sometimes within a few weeks. That's how this drug class works, and it's no reason for shame. If your dose has stopped working or you feel unwell between doses, talk to a physician.

Mixing benzodiazepines with opioids or alcohol sharply raises the risk of dangerous sedation and overdose.

  • You need a higher dose to get the same calm.
  • You feel shaky or irritable when a dose is late.
  • You run out early, or see more than one doctor to keep a supply.
  • You tried to cut down and got scared by what happened.

Can I just stop taking Xanax on my own?

Please don't. Stopping any benzodiazepine suddenly can be medically dangerous. Withdrawal can cause seizures, especially after high doses or long-term use. Anxiety and sleeplessness often rush back too, and that can make a solo attempt fall apart within days. The safe way off is a gradual taper that a physician designs and watches.

Medication management

Our psychiatric team meets with you regularly through the taper and treats anxiety or panic with safer, non-addictive options.

Cognitive behavioral therapy

You learn to spot catastrophic thinking and ride out the body's anxiety signals without a pill in your pocket.

Sleep help

Behavioral sleep strategies for the insomnia that often surges during a taper.

Dual diagnosis

Panic disorder, anxiety, PTSD and depression often sit underneath. We treat them along with the dependence.

How long does a benzo taper take?

Usually weeks to several months. It depends on your dose, how long you've taken it, which benzo it is and how your body responds. A slower taper is usually the more comfortable one. Your physician sets the pace with you, and there's no prize for finishing fast.

  1. Start

    Free assessment

    By phone or in person. We go over your dose and how long you've been taking it.

  2. Weeks

    Small dose cuts

    Your physician lowers the dose bit by bit and checks in with you regularly.

  3. Months

    Paced to you

    If symptoms flare, the plan slows down. It follows your body, never a calender.

  4. All along

    Therapy beside it

    CBT and groups for the anxiety and sleep problems that tend to surge during a taper.

Will group therapy help with benzo dependence?

For many people, yes. Benzo dependence can be isolating, and a lot of our clients have never met anyone else going through it. In group, people share what helps during a taper and slowly rebuild the social confidence that long-term sedative use wears down.

Does insurance pay for benzo treatment?

Most major plans may cover it. Federal parity law and California's SB 855 require plans that cover medical care to cover medically necessary addiction treatment on similar terms. We work with most major PPO plans and check your benefits for free, usually within the hour.

  • What your plan pays for a taper and therapy

Do you offer benzo detox on-site?

No. We're an outpatient center, so detox doesn't happen here. Many people can taper safely with our physicians while living at home. If your assessment shows you need medical detox first, usually after high doses or long-term use, we set it up with a trusted detox provider and hold your spot.

  1. Medical detox

    Only if your assessment calls for it. A trusted partner handles it.

  2. PHP

    About 6 hours a day on weekdays. You sleep at home.

  3. IOP

    3-hour sessions on a morning or evening track, so you can keep working.

  4. Alumni

    Weekly alumni groups and monthly check-ins for one full year.
