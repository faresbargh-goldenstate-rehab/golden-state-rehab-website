What is trauma-informed therapy?

Trauma-informed therapy is a way of running all care, from your first phone call to your last group. It starts from one idea: trauma may be part of your story, even if you've never called it that. Every part of treatment here is set up so it never makes things worse.

Is trauma-informed therapy the same as EMDR?

No. EMDR is one specific technique for working through hard memories. Trauma-informed care is the frame around your whole treatment, whatever method gets used inside it. Here we deliver CBT and DBT inside that frame, paced to your history.

Do I have to talk about my trauma?

No. You decide what to share and when. Many people start treatment without talking about their trauma at all. The early weeks focus on feeling steady. Deeper work starts only when you choose it and have the tools to do it safely.

Grounding and emotion regulation skills come first, so you have something to lean on when feelings spike.

What does trauma-informed care look like day to day?

It shows up in small things. Your clinicians explain the reason behind each part of your care, and your plan is built with your input. Trauma often takes power away from people, so here you get a say in your own treatment.

You control the pace

Nobody makes you retell your story before you're ready. Pushing too fast can do harm.

No surprises

You always know what's happening in your care and why. Nothing is decided behind closed doors.

A plan made with you

You're the expert on your own life. Your clinicians work as your partners.

A safe place

A calm, predictable enviroment so your nervous system can finally stand down.

How is trauma tied to my drinking or using?

For many people, substances started as the most reliable relief they could find for pain nobody helped them carry. Alcohol can quiet a body that's always on alert, and opioids do something similar for pain that feels unbearable. When treatment takes the substance away and ignores the pain under it, relapse often follows. We work on both at once.

Is trauma-informed therapy right for me?

Everyone here gets trauma-informed care, since it runs through every program at our Westwood center. It helps most if you've lived through trauma or if past treatment felt like too much. A free assessment, by phone or in person, is the easiest place to start.

  • You've lived with PTSD or complex trauma, diagnosed or not.
  • You grew up in a home that didn't feel safe.
  • Your relapses tend to follow triggers or anniversaries.
  • You've felt pushed too hard in treatment before.
  • Anxiety or depression shows up alongside your drinking or using.

Does insurance pay for trauma-informed therapy?

Trauma-informed care is how we run our outpatient programs, so your plan looks at PHP or IOP. Many PPO plans may cover that care. We work with most major PPO plans and check your benefits for free, usually within the hour.

Under California's SB 855, health plans must cover medically necessary care for mental health conditions like PTSD, and for addiction.
