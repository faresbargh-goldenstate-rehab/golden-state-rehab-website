Psychiatry, Done Properly

It Starts With Getting the Diagnosis Right

Many people who walk through our doors have spent years being treated for the wrong thing — anxiety medication for what was actually trauma, willpower lectures for what was actually depression, or no diagnosis at all because nobody ever sat with them long enough to ask the right questions. Psychiatric care at Golden State Rehab starts by fixing that: a thorough psychiatric evaluation at the beginning of treatment, conducted by a physician, before any plan is written.

From that evaluation comes an accurate diagnosis and a treatment plan built around it — one plan, not two. Your psychiatrist and your therapist work from the same chart, share notes internally, and adjust the plan together as you progress. Psychiatry here isn't a fifteen-minute appointment bolted onto the side of therapy; it's woven into it. Our psychiatric services are led by our Medical Director, Dr. Eric Chaghouri, MD, a board-certified psychiatrist who specializes in co-occurring psychiatric and addictive disorders.

Whether you're coming to us for depression, anxiety, trauma, substance use, or some combination you haven't fully untangled yet, psychiatric care is available at every level of our outpatient programs — in person at our Westwood center or by secure video anywhere in California.

The Full Picture

What Psychiatric Care Includes

Comprehensive Psychiatric Evaluation

A thorough, unhurried assessment at the start of treatment — your history, your symptoms, past diagnoses and medications, what's helped, and what hasn't. This is where we catch the things that were missed or mislabeled along the way, and it shapes everything that follows.

Diagnosis & Treatment Planning

An accurate diagnosis is the foundation of effective treatment. Your psychiatrist translates the evaluation into a clear, written treatment plan — what we're treating, how, and what progress should look like — and revisits it with you as things change rather than setting it once and forgetting it.

Medication Management

When medication is part of your plan, our medication management program handles the ongoing side — prescribing, monitoring, and adjusting doses over regular follow-up visits so your medications keep working as your recovery progresses.

Coordination With Your Therapist

Your psychiatrist and your primary therapist share notes internally and work from one treatment plan. What comes up in therapy informs psychiatric decisions, and vice versa — so you never have to be the messenger between two providers who don't talk to each other.

Telepsychiatry by Secure Video

Psychiatric appointments are available through secure telehealth anywhere in California — the same physician, the same care, without the drive. Many clients mix in-person and video visits depending on the week.

Care for Co-Occurring Conditions

Substance use and mental health conditions usually arrive together, and treating one while ignoring the other rarely holds. Our psychiatric team specializes in dual diagnosis treatment — addressing both sides of the picture in a single, coordinated plan.

What We Treat

Conditions Our Psychiatric Team Treats

Psychiatric care is part of treatment across our programs — whether your primary concern is mental health, substance use, or both at once:

  • Depression — including depression that hasn't responded to previous treatment attempts
  • Anxiety disorders — generalized anxiety, panic, and the anxiety that fuels substance use
  • PTSD — post-traumatic stress from single events or sustained experiences
  • Complex trauma — the layered effects of prolonged or repeated trauma, often misdiagnosed for years
  • Substance use disorders alongside mental health conditions — treated together, in one plan

One honest note: we are an outpatient center. If you or someone you love is in a psychiatric emergency — thoughts of suicide with a plan, psychosis, or immediate danger — the right first step is an emergency room or calling or texting 988, not an outpatient appointment. We're the ongoing-care side: once things are stable, we're here for the steady, week-over-week work of actually getting well.

Psychiatric Care: Common Questions

No referral is needed to start treatment with us. Psychiatric evaluation is built into admission to our programs — you call, we verify your insurance, and the evaluation is scheduled as part of getting started. Some insurance plans have their own referral rules for standalone psychiatric visits, which is one of the things our admissions team checks when they verify your benefits.

It's a structured conversation with a physician, not an interrogation. You'll talk through your current symptoms, your history — mental health, substance use, medical, family — any past diagnoses and medications, and what you want to be different. The psychiatrist uses that to form a diagnosis and a written treatment plan, which is reviewed with you before anything begins. Come as you are; you don't need to prepare anything beyond a list of current medications if you take any.

Some people benefit significantly from medication, some don't need it, and some try it and later taper off under supervision. If your psychiatrist believes medication could help, they'll explain why, what to expect, and the alternatives — and the decision stays yours. Plenty of our clients complete treatment without any psychiatric medication at all.
