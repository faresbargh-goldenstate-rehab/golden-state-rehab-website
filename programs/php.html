What is PHP?

PHP stands for partial hospitalization program. The name sounds like a hospital stay, but you never sleep here. You spend about six hours a day in therapy and groups at our Westwood center, Monday to Friday, then go home or back to sober living. PHP usually lasts two to four weeks, then you step down.

Is PHP right for me?

PHP usually makes sense when weekly therapy isn't enough anymore. It's the right fit when you need structure every day and more support than a weekly session, but you don't need to live at a facility.

  • You're stepping down from detox, residential care or a hospital stay.
  • You're dealing with addiction and a mental health condition at the same time.
  • You've tried weekly therapy and it hasn't been enough.
  • Things at home or work are slipping and you need structure every day.

If PHP is more than you need, we'll say so

Your free assessment decides the level of care. If IOP or weekly therapy fits better, that's what we'll reccomend. If you need detox first, we set it up with a trusted detox provider and hold your spot.

What does a day in PHP look like?

It runs from about 9am to 3pm. Most of the day is group therapy, plus time one on one with your own therapist.

  1. 9:00

    Check-in group

    Everyone says how they're doing and what's on their mind.

  2. 10:00

    Individual or family therapy

    One on one with your own therapist.

  3. 11:30

    Skills group

    CBT, DBT or trauma-focused tools for cravings and hard feelings.

  4. 1:00

    Process group

    An open group where people talk things through and give each other feedback.

  5. 2:00

    Education or holistic session

    Relapse prevention, family systems, mental health, or wellness practices.

  6. 3:00

    Wrap-up

    A short mindfulness practice, then you head home.

For most people, insurance does the heavy lifting. We work with most major PPO plans, and we check your benefits for free before you start.

California's SB 855 and federal parity law require health plans to cover medically necessary mental health and addiction care.

What about my job and my privacy?

PHP usually means a few weeks off work. Federal FMLA and California's CFRA usually protect your job for a serious health condition, and your leave request doesn't have to say why. Your records are protected by HIPAA and 42 CFR Part 2, and we never contact your employer.

Your job

Plan to step away for the length of PHP, usually two to four weeks. Once you're steadier, IOP has an evening track built around a full-time job. How FMLA works

Your privacy

42 CFR Part 2 is a federal privacy rule written just for addiction treatment. Nothing is shared without your written consent, outside of rare legal exceptions.

Your family

Family therapy can be part of your week, and we offer family sessions in Spanish too.

What happens after PHP?

As things get steadier, most people move to IOP, with 3-hour sessions 3 to 5 days a week, mornings or evenings. After that, weekly alumni groups and monthly check-ins keep you connected for a year.

  1. Detox

    Only if you need it first. We set it up with a partner.

  2. You're here

    PHP

    About 6 hours a day, 5 days a week.

  3. IOP

    3-hour sessions, 3 to 5 days a week. Mornings or evenings.

  4. Alumni

    Weekly alumni groups and monthly check-ins for a full year.
